FEATURES

EVERY EXCHANGE IS A DECISION

LOCKDOWN turns a BJJ match into a turn-based battle of positions, grips and gas tanks. No button mashing — just you, your opponent, and the plan.

🗺️

MAP & PLAN

Open MAP & PLAN mid-fight to see which moves chain toward a finish. Plan two steps ahead and turn a scramble into a checkmate.

🔋

STAMINA IS A WEAPON

Watch your stamina bar and fight for grips. Every action costs gas — and gassed opponents are easy opponents.

🏆

STORY CAMPAIGN

Battle through the story campaign and take down the act bosses. Beat the right opponents and they join your roster.

🥋

UNLOCKABLE FIGHTERS

Earn Rusty, Yuki and Mahmedov by proving yourself on the mats. Each fighter brings their own style to the game.

📱

FULLY OFFLINE

No account, no server, no signal required. Campaign slots, unlocked fighters, levels and high scores are saved right on your device.

🔓

ONE-TIME UNLOCK

No subscriptions. A single optional purchase unlocks the full game — restore it on any device with your Apple ID.
