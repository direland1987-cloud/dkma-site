THE FIGHT

REAL GRAPPLING, TURNED INTO A GAME

LOCKDOWN turns a Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu match into a turn-based battle of positions, grips and gas tanks. Every move is a real technique — and every decision matters.

🥋

11 Positions

Fight across authentic positions — standing, clinch, the guards, mounts, back control and leg entanglements. Positions chain the way they do on the mats.

🎯

Skill, Not Dice

Timing and reaction mini-games decide whether your move lands. Takedowns, sweeps, passes, chokes, armlocks and leg attacks — all earned, never rolled.

🔋

Stamina Is The Fight

Gas your opponent out, protect your own tank, and strike when they fade. Build your special meter as you fight and unleash signature techniques.

📖

5-Act Career

Rise from white-belt nobody through rival gyms to the final boss. Win to unlock new playable fighters, each with their own stats, style and signature moves.

🌳

Level Up Your Game

Level your fighter, unlock new moves Pokémon-style and build custom loadouts per position. A campaign-only skill tree — arcade stays a pure test of skill.

📱

No Strings

Fully playable offline. No account and no subscription — one optional purchase unlocks the whole game. Restore it on any device with your Apple ID.
