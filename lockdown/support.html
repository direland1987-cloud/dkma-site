LOCKDOWN — Support

Turn-based BJJ fighting for iPhone.

Contact

For bugs, questions or feedback, email admin@dkma.online. Include your iPhone model and iOS version if you're reporting a bug — screenshots help a lot.

Frequently asked

I bought the full unlock on my old phone — how do I get it back?
Open the unlock screen and tap Restore Purchase. Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID you bought it with. No account or code needed — Apple handles it.

No sound?
Check the in-game Settings (gear icon on the main menu) — music and effects have separate volume sliders and mute buttons. The game plays audio even with the ringer switch on silent, like other games do.

Where is my save?
Progress is stored on your device automatically — campaign slots, unlocked fighters, levels and high scores. Deleting the app deletes your progress, so unlock-worthy runs are best kept installed. There is no cloud sync in this version.

How do I unlock Yuki, Rusty and Mahmedov?
Beat the right opponents in the story campaign. Rusty falls first — take down the Act 1 boss and he joins your roster.

The game feels hard.
Good. But: watch your stamina bar, fight for grips, and use MAP & PLAN mid-fight to see which moves chain toward a finish. Gassed opponents are easy opponents.
