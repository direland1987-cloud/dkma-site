DATA NOT COLLECTED

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 July 2026

The short version: LOCKDOWN collects no data. None.

What we collect

Nothing. LOCKDOWN has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking technologies and no server of its own. The game runs entirely on your device and is fully playable offline.

What stays on your device

Your game progress — saves, unlocked fighters, high scores, settings — is stored locally on your device only. It is never transmitted to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Purchases

The optional one-time in-app purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details. Purchases can be restored on a new device through Apple's standard Restore Purchases mechanism.

Children

Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we also collect none from children.

Changes

If a future update ever changes any of the above (for example, adding online leaderboards), this policy will be updated first and the change will be listed in the App Store release notes.
